How each day is counted
  1. Public holiday or in-lieu day: no leave.
  2. Rest day under your work week: no leave.
  3. Office closure: no leave, unless the closure is set to use annual leave. A closure that lands on a holiday or rest day never costs leave.
  4. Any other day: one day of annual leave.

In-lieu days are derived, not typed in. When a holiday falls on a rest day, the next working day that isn't already a holiday becomes the day in lieu. The rule per country is a setting in the Data tab. Your HR team sets this in data/settings.json to match company policy.

About this data

Holiday dates are maintained by your HR team and every change is recorded in the project’s history. Projected dates, usually those that depend on a moon sighting, show as hatched until they are confirmed and may still move. This planner doesn’t submit or approve leave, and it doesn’t know your leave balance. Apply through your company’s leave system.
